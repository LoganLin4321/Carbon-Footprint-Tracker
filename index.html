How this estimate works: We add gasoline-car tailpipe emissions (EPA average: 0.40 kg CO₂/mile), electric-car charging (assumed 0.30 kWh/mile), food-production emissions, hot-water shower energy, and lighting. Electricity uses the U.S. EPA eGRID 2022 national-average rate (823.1 lb CO₂/MWh, about 0.373 kg/kWh). Shower estimates assume an EPA WaterSense-rate 2.0 gpm showerhead, electric water heating, and a 45°F temperature rise; lighting assumes one 10W LED bulb. This is a learning tool, not a full lifecycle footprint or personal benchmark. EPA vehicle factor · EPA WaterSense showerheads · Our World in Data food factors · EPA eGRID methodology
Approximate food factors per kg: beef 99, pork 12, poultry 9.9, farmed fish 14, mixed plant foods 2.5 kg CO₂e. The mixed plant-food value is a rough proxy. Totals omit public transit/air travel, non-electric home energy, water treatment, and food-waste treatment. Actual emissions vary with vehicle efficiency, local electricity, shower fixtures, and water heating. Your data stays in local browser storage and is not uploaded.
